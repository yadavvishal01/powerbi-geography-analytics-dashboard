# Geography Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Summary

This Power BI project analyses geographic and market performance across regions, states, cities, city tiers, store formats, categories, brands, and SKUs. The dashboard provides both regional overview and city-level shopping behaviour analysis.

## Business Problem

The business needs to understand which geographic markets contribute most to revenue, how performance differs across city tiers and store formats, and which categories, brands, and SKUs drive sales.

## Key Work Performed

* Built regional overview and city shopping behaviour dashboards.
* Analysed sales, transactions, ATV, quantity, customers, and spend per customer.
* Compared performance across states, cities, and city tiers.
* Analysed store format contribution.
* Identified top categories based on sales and YoY growth.
* Compared brands using sales, contribution, growth, SKU count, and sales per SKU.
* Analysed top-performing SKUs and category trends.

## Important Insights / Findings

* North region recorded CY Sales of ₹97.31M with 24.6% YoY growth.
* CY Transactions were 113K, with 21.0% YoY growth.
* Uttar Pradesh contributed 38.93% of regional sales, while Rajasthan showed an ATV of ₹1,008.1.
* T1 cities contributed 56.09% of sales, followed by T2 cities at 35.27%.
* Supermarkets contributed 81.27% of regional sales.
* In the selected Chandigarh analysis, Rice & Grains was the highest-sales category shown, with ₹2.9M sales.
* In Rice & Grains, Daawat contributed 33.11% of category sales.

## Business Impact / Interpretation

The dashboard helps identify high-contributing geographic markets and understand differences in city, store-format, category, brand, and SKU performance for further business analysis.
